Standards mapping Controls this register serves

ReferenceControlHow it is met
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.9Inventory of information and other associated assetsEvery connector, skill and repository is enumerated with an owner, a status and a unique asset ID.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.19Information security in supplier relationshipsConnectors are third-party processors; each carries a risk tier and a stated data exposure.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.23Information security for use of cloud servicesAuthorisation state is recorded per connector and re-verified each monthly run.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.9Configuration managementSkills and plugins are version-tracked; the monthly snapshot is immutable and diffed.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.16Monitoring activitiesUsage telemetry is ingested into an append-only ledger keyed on event UUID.
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.8.28 / A.8.31Secure coding; separation of environmentsRepository visibility is recorded, and public repositories are flagged Tier 1.
