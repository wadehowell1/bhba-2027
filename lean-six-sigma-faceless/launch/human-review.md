# Human Review Register

Every decision in this project that a person has to make. Nothing here can be resolved
by more analysis — each one needs a judgment, a purchase, a professional, or a
conversation with a real customer.

**Ordered by what blocks what.**

---

## Questions only a customer can answer

No amount of further analysis substitutes for these. They are the content of Test 4.

1. What did you already try to fix this, and what did it cost?
2. Would you buy process help with no certificate attached?
3. Does "one process, 30 days, two hours a week" sound small enough to say yes to?
4. Who signs off on a $2,400 spend in your business?
5. Does a brand with no face bother you when you are buying advice?

## What this project is, and what it is not

**It is:** a complete, internally consistent business plan built from one paragraph of
intent and public sources, with the evidence separated from the assumptions and the
gaps named rather than filled in with plausible-sounding numbers.

**It is not:** validated. Not one customer has been spoken to. Every price is a guess.
The market research rests on published sources, none of which are about your specific
vertical or your specific town.

The most valuable thing here is not the plan. It is the validation sequence — the part
that tells you the cheapest order in which to find out whether the plan is wrong.
