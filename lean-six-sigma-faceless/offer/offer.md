# The Offer

> Positioning in one line: **Lean, translated for small business.**
> We do not sell belts. We fix one process at a time.

Working brand name: **The Lean Desk** — see `positioning.md` for alternatives and the
naming rationale. Domain and trademark checks are **not** done; flagged for human
review.

---

## OFFER NAME

### **Fix One Process**

The name is the offer. It states the scope, sets the expectation, and pre-answers the
biggest objection ("I don't have time") before it is raised.

---

## TARGET CUSTOMER

Owner or GM of a 5–50 person service or light-production business, where the same job
runs many times a week and runs differently every time. Full profile:
`../research/ideal-customer-profile.md`.

## CORE PROBLEM

The same faults keep happening — rework, callbacks, things dropped between people,
one person who is the only one who knows — and the owner has no method to fix it, no
time to learn one, and no slack in the team to run a change programme.

## PROMISED OUTCOME

> **One process in your business that runs the same way every time, without you in
> the room — in 30 days, working on it about two hours a week.**

Note what is deliberately absent: no percentage savings, no revenue claims, no
"transform your business." We do not have the evidence for those yet, and the project
rules forbid inventing it. When we have measured results from real engagements, they
go here, attributed.

## RUNG 0 — The 60-Minute Waste Walk *(free)*

**What it is:** a structured one-hour walk through a single process, with a worksheet
that ends in a ranked list of where the time and money actually go.

**Why it exists:** the research says small business owners have no slack and no
method, and that lean fails when it arrives as a programme. So the first contact must
cost one hour and produce something real. It also proves the method on their own
business rather than on a Toyota case study.

Full content: `../marketing/lead-magnet/waste-walk.md`. It is genuinely complete —
someone can run it, get value, and never buy anything.

## RUNG 1 — The Small Shop Lean Kit *($49)*

**What it is:** the core lean toolset rewritten for a business with eleven people.

**Deliverables:**
1. Process Map (one page — not a 40-symbol swimlane)
2. Waste Walk Worksheet (extended version)
3. Standard Work sheet — with a filled-in example
4. Daily Huddle board and 10-minute agenda
5. Problem One-Pager (an A3, without the word "A3" on the front)
6. 5 Whys / root cause sheet — with a worked small-business example
7. Job Setup Checklist (error-proofing in plain language)
8. Improvement Tracker (one sheet, not a PMO)
9. **The Translation Guide** — every lean term, what it actually means, and whether
   you ever need to say it out loud

**Why it exists, and why it can charge anything at all:** the research is explicit
that comparable templates are sold cheaply and given away free. The artifacts are a
commodity. What is not a commodity is that **every one of these is already filled in
with a small-business example**, sequenced in the order you would actually use them,
and stripped of enterprise vocabulary — directly addressing the documented training
and expertise gap that makes blank templates useless to this buyer.

**Format:** Google Sheets / Docs (copyable), plus PDF. Deliberately **not** a 300-slide
PowerPoint — that is what the competition ships.

## RUNG 2 — Fix One Process *($349)*

**What it is:** a 30-day self-paced system that takes one real process from "however
Dave does it" to documented, measured, and running without the owner.

**The product is the finished process, not the lessons.** Every module ends with a
completed artifact for their business.

| Week | They do | They end with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick the process, measure what happens now | A baseline number and a one-page map |
| 2 | Find where the time and errors actually go | A ranked cause list with evidence |
| 3 | Design and pilot the new way | Standard work, tested by the person doing the job |
| 4 | Make it stick and prove it moved | Huddle rhythm, re-measured baseline, before/after |

**Why 30 days:** long enough to change something real, short enough that an owner can
see the end from the start. **Why two hours a week:** because the research says the
team has no slack, and a programme demanding more will be abandoned in week two.

**Included:** all Kit templates, worked examples, short video walkthroughs, and a
"what if it goes wrong" troubleshooting section for the five most common stalls.

> ⚠️ **Build this last.** It is the most expensive thing here to produce and should
> not be built until Gates 2–4 of the validation plan pass.

## RUNG 3 — The 90-Day Desk *($2,400 / 90 days)*

**What it is:** done-with-you coaching. Fortnightly working sessions, not lectures.
We fix the first process together, then a second one with the owner leading, then
hand over the rhythm.

**Deliverables:**
- Kick-off diagnostic: the three processes costing the most, ranked, with rough
  numbers
- 6 × 60-minute fortnightly working sessions
- Two processes fixed — the first led by us, the second led by them
- A running daily or weekly huddle their team actually uses
- A before/after measure on both processes
- A 90-day handover plan so improvement continues without us

**Delivery is by a named human.** The brand is faceless; the coaching is not. See the
faceless-scope decision in `../business-plan.md` §8.

**Capacity:** ⚠️ hard-capped. Assumption: 4–6 concurrent engagements maximum for one
person. This tier does not scale, and pretending otherwise is how service businesses
drown.

## OBJECTIONS AND HONEST RESPONSES

**"I don't have time for this."**
That is the reason to do it, and we know it is not a satisfying answer on its own. So
the scope is built around it: one hour for the Waste Walk, two hours a week for 30
days. If you cannot find two hours a week, this will not work and we would rather you
kept the $349.

**"Who are you?"** *(the faceless objection — the hardest one)*
The brand publishes without a face because the material should stand on its own. The
coaching does not — you will know exactly who is in the room with you before you pay
anything. And the free Waste Walk exists precisely so you can judge the method rather
than the marketing.
> ⚠️ **This answer is only honest if the coaching tier genuinely names a real,
> qualified person.** Flagged for human review.

**"Isn't this just cost-cutting? I'm not laying anyone off."**
No — and if that is your goal, we are the wrong people. Every process we fix is aimed
at rework, waiting and mistakes, not at headcount. The research on why lean fails in
small firms names this exact misreading as a primary cause. Your team has to want it
to work, and they will not if they think it is aimed at them.

**"My business is different."**
Partly true — the specifics always are. The pattern usually is not. The Waste Walk is
free precisely so you can test that claim on your own process before spending
anything.

**"Can't I get these templates free?"**
Yes, and we will point you at good free libraries. What you are paying for is that
ours are filled in, sequenced, and written for your size of business. If a blank
template was enough, you would already have fixed this.

**"What results should I expect?"**
We do not have published results yet, and we are not going to invent any. What we can
tell you is the method, the scope, and exactly what you will have at the end. When we
have measured results from real clients, they will appear here with names attached.
