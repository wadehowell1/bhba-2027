# Pricing Worksheet

> 🚨 **Read this first.** Every number on this page is an **assumption**. None is
> derived from a sale, a quote, or a measured cost. They are here to be tested and
> replaced, not to be believed. Test 2 (competitor quotes) and Test 5 (real
> checkout) in `../research/validation-plan.md` are what turn these into facts.

---

## Where the price anchors come from

The only **observed** pricing in this market is on the certification side:

| Observed (verified) | Price |
|---|---|
| Self-paced online Green Belt courses | from ~$300 |
| University online Green Belt (8 weeks, self-paced) | $1,995 |
| University in-person Green Belt (5 days) | $2,495 |
| Certification exam | ~$295 |
| ASQ Green Belt exam re-attempt | $269 |
| IASSC renewal | $175 |

Source detail: `../research/market-research.md#sources`.

**Consulting and retainer pricing could not be observed at all** — the firms serving
SMEs advertise retainers but publish no prices (C1). Our highest-value tier is
therefore the **least** anchored, which is exactly backwards from where you want your
uncertainty. Test 2 exists to fix this.

### How we use the anchor

The buyer's mental reference for "lean, a few hundred to ~$2,500" is a **credential**.
Our $349 tier sits inside that band while selling something different — a fixed
process rather than a certificate. That is either the smartest thing here or the
weakest: it means the comparison will be made, so the copy must draw the distinction
before the buyer draws it for us.

## Rung 1 — The Small Shop Lean Kit

| Line | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **$49** | 🔶 Assumption |
| Estimated cost to deliver | ~$1.90/sale | Payment fees ~$1.72 (2.9%+$0.30) + hosting/delivery |
| Gross margin | ~96% | Derived |

| CAC assumption | $0 organic / $15–25 paid | ❓ Pure guess — no ad data |
| Break-even (build only, at cost of time $0) | ~1 sale to cover fees; **~25–40 sales** to justify the build time at $50/hr notional | Derived from above |
| Revenue type | One-time | — |

**Pricing logic:** $49 sits above impulse-but-disposable and below "I need approval."
It is a personal-wallet purchase for the Practitioner and a rounding error for the
Operator. Deliberately **not** $19 — at that price the perceived value collapses toward
the free libraries we are trying to differentiate from.

> **Risk:** free comprehensive template libraries exist and are good. If the
> translation-and-worked-examples difference is not immediately visible **on the
> sales page**, $49 is unsellable. The page must show the filled-in example, not
> describe it.

| Line | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|

| Capacity | 4–6 concurrent | 🔶 Assumption |

**Note the two margin lines.** The 95% figure is the one that flatters; the 41% figure

> **Risk:** entirely unanchored (C1). It could be 40% under market or 40% over.
> **Do not print this price publicly until Test 2 returns real competitor quotes.**

## Revenue mix modelling — *illustrative arithmetic only*

> 🚨 These are **not forecasts.** No conversion rate here has been observed. They
> exist to show what would have to be true, so you can judge whether that is
> plausible — not to predict revenue. Treat every input as a dial to test.

**Scenario A — modest, month 12**

| Input (all assumed) | Value |
|---|---|
| Email list | 2,000 |
| Kit conversion, monthly | 1.5% → 30 sales |
| Course conversion, monthly | 0.3% → 6 sales |
| Coaching | 2 concurrent |

| Line | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Kit — 30 × $49 | $1,470 |
| Course — 6 × $349 | $2,094 |
| Coaching — 2 × $800/mo equivalent | $1,600 |
| **Gross revenue** | **$5,164** |
| Less processing (~3%) | −$155 |
| Less tools/hosting/email | −$150 |
| **Contribution before founder time** | **~$4,859** |

**What has to be true for Scenario A:** a 2,000-person list of the right people, and
sustained content output for ~12 months. Both are large, unproven inputs.

**Scenario B — floor case.** List grows to 500, Kit converts at 0.8%, no course, one
coaching client: roughly **$996/month**. This is the realistic early state and it is
worth planning around, because it is what months 3–8 probably look like.

## Discounting policy

- **No launch discounts on the Kit.** At $49 there is nothing to discount.
- **Pilot pricing on Rung 3 is allowed once** — explicitly as a pilot, in exchange
  for a documented before/after and permission to publish it (Test 6). Say plainly
  that it is a pilot rate; do not fake a "normal price" it has never sold at.
- **Never invent a struck-through price.** Showing "$997 ~~$1,997~~" for something
  that has never sold at $1,997 is a fabricated claim.

## Things that will change these numbers

1. Test 2 competitor quotes → repricing Rung 3, probably significantly.
2. Test 5 real conversion → repricing Rung 1 or rebuilding the sales page.
3. Choosing a vertical (C4) → higher prices are defensible in a narrow niche.
4. **Payment processing.** ⚠️ Stripe is unavailable in Jamaica, so a merchant of
   record is required. MoR fees run materially higher than Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30 —
   Lemon Squeezy publishes 5% + $0.50, for example (⚠️ verify current rates). Every
   cost-to-deliver line below assumes Stripe-like fees and is therefore **understated**.
   At $49 the real fee is roughly $3 rather than $1.72; at $349, roughly $18 rather
   than $10.42. Margins stay high, but re-run these once the platform is chosen.
5. **US sales tax** on digital goods is largely handled by the MoR as seller of record.
   Confirm residual obligations with an accountant.
