## PROBLEM SECTION

**Heading:** You already know where it hurts

You have had this week before.

The job that got done twice because nobody wrote down how it was done the first time.
The customer who found the mistake before you did. The quote that was wrong because
the information was in someone's head, or someone's van, or someone's inbox. The
Tuesday that disappeared into questions only you can answer.

None of it is a disaster. That is the problem. It is small, it is constant, and it is
expensive in a way that never shows up as a line on the accounts.

And the advice out there does not fit. It assumes a plant, a budget, a spare team and
a six-month program. You have none of those. You have Thursday.

## SOLUTION SECTION

**Heading:** Fix one process. Properly. Then the next one.

There is a proven method for this. It is called lean, and it works — it is just that
almost everything written about it was written for large manufacturers. The published
research on why lean fails in small businesses says exactly that: the roadmaps were
built for big companies, and small firms cannot run them with the time, money and
expertise they actually have.

So we changed the packaging, not the method.

**One process at a time.** Not a transformation. Not a program. One thing that keeps
going wrong, fixed so it stops.

**Two hours a week.** Because you do not have five.

**Every template already filled in.** A blank template is not help. You will see a
worked example from a business the size of yours before you fill in anything.

**Your words on the outside.** The method has its own vocabulary. You never have to
use it. It's in there if you want it — in a guide that translates every term into
plain English.

## FAQ

**Do I get a certification?**
No. We do not sell one and we are not accredited to. If a credential is what you need,
Green Belt courses run from a few hundred dollars to around $2,500 and there are
several reputable providers. What we sell is a process that works, which is a
different purchase.

**Is this just a cost-cutting exercise?**
No, and it will not work if it is. The research on why lean fails in small businesses
names this exact misreading as a leading cause: the moment a team believes an
improvement effort is aimed at their jobs, it stops working. We aim at rework, waiting
and mistakes. If your goal is fewer people, we are the wrong people.

**I genuinely do not have time.**
That is the objection and the symptom. It is also why the scope is what it is: one
hour for the Waste Walk, two hours a week after that. If you cannot find two hours a
week, this will not work, and we would rather you kept your money.

**Why is there nobody's face on this?**
Because the material should stand up on its own. The free Waste Walk exists so you can
judge the method on your own business before you trust anyone. Coaching is different —
you will know exactly who is in the room with you before you pay for anything.

**Can I get these templates for free somewhere?**
Some of them, yes, and there are good free libraries out there. What you are paying
for is that ours are filled in, sequenced, and written for a business your size. If a
blank template were enough, this would already be fixed.

**What results should I expect?**
We are not going to give you a number we have not measured. We can tell you the
method, the scope, and exactly what you will have at the end of four weeks. When we
have measured results from real clients, they will appear here with their names on
them.

**Will this work for my industry?**
It works where the same job runs many times a week and runs differently every time.
That covers a lot of trades, clinics, agencies, workshops and fulfillment operations.
The Waste Walk is free specifically so you can test that on your own process rather
than take our word for it.

## ⚠️ Proof placeholders — read before publishing

The site contains a section reserved for social proof. It currently states plainly
that there are no published results yet. **That is intentional and it must stay that
way until Test 6 produces a real, permissioned case study.**

Do not add: invented testimonials, stock-photo "clients", logo bars for companies that
are not clients, "join 1,000+ owners" before there are 1,000, or made-up percentages.
Every one of those is a fabricated claim, and in this market it is also the exact
signal your buyer has learned to distrust.
